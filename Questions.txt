Undergraduate Question 1
Your recipe page must remain readable at a 320px viewport without horizontal scrolling. 
Using the actual content in your starter file, determine whether the ingredients should use a row, a wrapping row, or a column at that width. 
Predict what will happen to the widest item, then defend your choice by tracing the effects of flex-direction, flex-wrap, flex-basis, and available width. 
State one tradeoff your solution creates at a wider viewport.

/* Answer */
At a 320px viewport, the ingredients should use a wrapping row, so the items can move to another line when there is not enough space horizontally. 
The widest ingredient would most likely wrap onto its own line rather than forcing the page to scroll horizontally. 
flex-basis determines how much space each item needs, while flex-wrap makes it so that items that exceed the available width can move to the next line. 
The main tradeoff is that at wider viewports, wrapping could create multiple rows or unused horizontal space.

Undergraduate Question 2
One Tic-Tac-Toe square contains a long label and causes the board to become wider than intended. 
Without changing the HTML, identify whether the fault is caused by the grid container, the grid track definition, or the square's internal alignment. 
Use a browser observation to support your diagnosis, propose the smallest CSS correction, and explain why the correction preserves a centered 3x3 board.

/* Answer2 */
The problem is caused by the grid track definition. 
In the browser we can see the column get wider because of the content size.
The smallest correction would be to use grid-template-columns: repeat(3, minmax(0, 1fr));, 
which would allow for each column to shrink while still dividing the available space equally. 
This stops the long label from expanding the board and preserves a centered, evenly sized 3×3 grid.
